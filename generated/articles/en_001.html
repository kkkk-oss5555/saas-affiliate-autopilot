If managing separate page and email tools is slowing you down, start with a small test you can actually finish. This guide focuses on a single testable sales funnel.

What to build first

  • Pick one offer and one audience. Write down the problem the offer solves.
  • Create one landing page with a clear headline, a short explanation and a single call to action.
  • Connect one welcome email. Explain what the visitor receives and what happens next.
  • Test the signup and every link yourself on a phone and a desktop browser.
  • Review visits, clicks and signups after a week before adding more tools.

A useful decision rule

Keep the first version if visitors understand the offer and complete the intended action. If they leave without clicking, rewrite the headline and simplify the page. If they click but do not sign up, check the form and the promise made by the call to action. Do not treat a small sample as proof of future income.
